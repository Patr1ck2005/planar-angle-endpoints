# An enclosed planar cluster

For every positive cluster size W, the least attainable cap on a cluster enclosed by the other sites is π−π/(ceil(log2 W)+3). All other weights and the number of centers are arbitrary. Equality is realized on exactly four sites with outer weights one. Conversely every attaining support has precisely four sites. The explicit lower and upper bounds, rigidity including the W=1 case, and the sharp value 5π/6 for W=5 are unconditional in Lean.

If five selected sites remain uniformly separated relative to their diameter and one of weight at least W is enclosed by the other four, a strictly positive angular gap above this minimum depends only on W and the separation ratio. The uniform gap is existential. A largest cluster enclosed on a five-, six-, seven- or eight-site support forces the entire distribution strictly above the Sendov target. For arbitrary support sizes the original problem is equivalent to the critical endpoint test with the additional condition that all sites whose capacity exponent is at least k are vertices of the full convex hull.

Every seven or more distinct ordinary planar points have an angle strictly greater than 2π/3. At N=7 and 8 the exact ordinary infimum is 2π/3 and neither infimum is attained. Seven points separated at a fixed fraction of the diameter force a positive uniform excess. The displayed sharp enclosed-cluster value is different from the ordinary infimum at the total number of labels.

The three-page manuscript enclosed-angle.pdf gives a direct sign-word proof independent of the Lean implementation. The public entry EnclosedResearch also imports the convex arc-mass and saturated-turn results at the 17- and 21-point endpoints. All these new geometric theorems are independent of SAT. The unrestricted seven-or-more-site ordinary endpoint problem remains open in this project.
